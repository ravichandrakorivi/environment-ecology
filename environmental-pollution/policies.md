# Sockholm Convention

**Q10. Why is DDT banned in Western countries?**

**A. It remains in the environment for a long time**  
**B. The insects are immunised**  
**C. It is a highly poisonous insecticide**  
**D. None of the above**

**[CSE 1987]**

**Answer: A**

>**Explanation:**
>DDT (Dichlorodiphenyltrichloroethane) is a persistent organochlorine insecticide that degrades very slowly in the environment.
>
>- Persistence: Remains in soil and other environmental compartments for long periods.
>- Bioaccumulation: Accumulates in the fatty tissues of organisms.
>- Biomagnification: Its concentration increases at successive trophic levels in food chains.
>- Ecological impact: Causes eggshell thinning in certain birds, affecting reproduction.
>
>UPSC Prelims Note: DDT is a Persistent Organic Pollutant (POP) regulated under the Stockholm Convention. Its use is prohibited or severely restricted in many countries, with limited exemptions for disease-vector control.
